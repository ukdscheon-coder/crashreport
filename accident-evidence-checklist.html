UK car accident evidence checklist

Collect evidence only when it is safe and lawful. Emergency care and road safety come first.

Scene

  • Time and exact or approximate location
  • Wide photographs showing vehicle positions
  • Road signs, markings, junctions and traffic lights
  • Weather, visibility and road surface
  • Debris, skid marks and relevant hazards

Vehicles

  • Registration plates
  • All sides of each vehicle
  • Close-ups and wider views of damage
  • Make, model and colour
  • Commercial vehicle or fleet identifiers, when relevant

People and documents

  • Driver and owner contact information
  • Insurer and policy details
  • Independent witness contact information
  • Police incident reference

Your account

Write a factual description while events are fresh. Separate what you directly saw from assumptions. Do not alter original photographs.
