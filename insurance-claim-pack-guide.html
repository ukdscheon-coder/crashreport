How to prepare an insurer-ready accident claim pack

A claim pack should help the insurer understand what happened and locate the supporting evidence without searching through unrelated messages and photographs.

Recommended structure

  1. Policyholder and vehicle information
  2. Incident date, time and location
  3. Short factual description
  4. Other parties and vehicles
  5. Witness information
  6. Police or incident reference
  7. Numbered photographs with short descriptions
  8. Relevant receipts or supporting records

Quality checks

  • Keep the original files
  • Use consistent dates and registration numbers
  • Remove duplicate or irrelevant images
  • Check contact details before submission
  • Follow the insurer’s own claim procedure
